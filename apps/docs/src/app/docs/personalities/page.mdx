## Choosing one

Pass `personality` to the provider. Sticker is the default.

```tsx
<PlonkProvider personality="retro">
  <App />
</PlonkProvider>
```

Providers nest, and the closest one wins. A settings page can be Retro inside a
Sticker app, or a single promo card can be Arcade:

```tsx
<PlonkProvider personality="sticker">
  <Dashboard />
  <PlonkProvider personality="arcade">
    <HighScores />
  </PlonkProvider>
</PlonkProvider>
```

Dialogs, menus and toasts opened inside a provider keep its personality, even
though they render at the end of the page. To read the current personality in
your own components, call `usePersonality()`.

## The four

### Sticker

Notebook lines, paper tape and stickers stuck on at a slight angle. Press a
button and it squashes, then springs back like jelly. Toasts drop in with a
strip of tape and peel off from the corner when dismissed. Calendar pages tear
off as you move forward.

### Retro

Every surface is a window with a striped title bar and a pixel font. Motion is
stepped and instant, like an old Mac: dialogs open with growing outline frames,
toasts roll up like a window shade, and pressing a button snaps without bounce.

### Comic

Thick ink, halftone dots and a hand-lettered body font. Motion is fast and
overshoots. Buttons shout **POW!** or **ZAP!** where you clicked, dialogs open as
speech bubbles, and dismissed toasts tear in half.

### Arcade

Domed buttons, neon glow and pixel steps. Pressing a button throws **+100** into
the air, lists open like an old CRT screen, and a dialog with a timer turns into
a giant **CONTINUE? 9…8…**

Arcade is the only dark personality. Its colors are tuned to pass contrast on
its own dark canvas, so give it its own surface instead of placing it on a light
page's background.

## Press effects

Comic and Arcade add an effect on top of the press: a sound word in Comic,
flying points in Arcade. Buttons play it on every press; checkboxes and switches
only when they turn on. Keyboard presses play it from the middle of the
control.

To turn effects off, set `--plonk-press-effect` to `none`, for one personality
or for a part of the page:

```css
/* No effects anywhere in Comic */
[data-plonk-personality="comic"] {
  --plonk-press-effect: none;
}

/* Or only in this toolbar */
.toolbar {
  --plonk-press-effect: none;
}
```

With reduced motion, effects only fade in and out in place.

## Tape

Decorative tape sits on toasts and on the data table's bulk action bar. Each
personality draws its own: paper tape in Sticker, a dithered pixel strip in
Retro, a blue halftone ribbon in Comic and a blinking **1UP** plate in Arcade.

<Example name="personalities/tape" title="Tape" toaster />

Turn it off everywhere with the provider. Nested providers inherit the setting:

```tsx
<PlonkProvider tape={false}>
  <App />
</PlonkProvider>
```

A component's own setting always wins over the provider's:

```tsx
toast("Saved", { tape: true });
```

The data table has its own switch for its bulk action bar: `bulkBarTape`.

Cards are the exception: they have no tape by default. Add it with `<Card tape>`.
