## Usage

```tsx
import { Card } from "plonk";

export function Report() {
  return (
    <Card title="Weekly report">
      Signups are up 12% since the new onboarding shipped.
    </Card>
  );
}
```

`Card` is a `<section>`. Every part is optional, and only the parts you pass
are rendered, so a card can be just a body or just a title.

### Anatomy

`title` and `description` head the card, `actions` sit to the right of the
title, `children` is the body and `footer` holds the buttons at the bottom.

<Example name="card/anatomy" title="Card · Anatomy" />

### Closable

Pass `onClose` to show a close box. The card doesn't hide itself; you decide
what closing means. Give the box a specific name with `closeLabel`.

<Example name="card/closable" title="Card · Closable" />

### Tilt

`tilt` sticks the card on at a slight angle, to the left or the right. The
angle is the personality's `--plonk-tilt`: 1.5° in Sticker, 0.8° in Comic, and
none in Retro and Arcade, which stay straight.

<Example name="card/tilt" title="Card · Tilt" />

## Tape

Cards have no tape by default. Add it with `tape`, and each personality draws
its own: paper tape in Sticker, a dithered pixel strip at the top right in
Retro, a blue halftone strip in Comic, and a blinking neon "1UP" plate in
Arcade.

```tsx
<Card tape title="Pinned note">
  Same prop, four kinds of tape.
</Card>
```

<TapeLineup />

The provider's `tape` setting turns tape off for toasts and the data table, but
never adds it to cards: a card only has tape when you ask for it. See
[Personalities](/docs/personalities#tape) for the global switch.

## Personalities

Sticker is a sheet of paper with a hard shadow. Retro wraps the card in a window
with a striped title bar, and the close box moves to the left like on an old
Mac. Comic prints a halftone corner and puts the title in a yellow caption box.
Arcade lights the title up like a high score.

## Accessibility

- A card with a `title` is a region named by its title, so screen reader users
  can find it in the landmarks list. Without a title it's a plain section.
- The title is an `<h3>`. Keep your page's headings in order around it.
- The close box is a real button with an accessible name, "Close" by default.
- Tape, halftone and title bar stripes are decoration and stay silent.
