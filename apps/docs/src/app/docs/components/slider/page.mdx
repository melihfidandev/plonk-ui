## Usage

```tsx
import { Slider } from "plonk-ui";

export function Volume() {
  return <Slider label="Volume" defaultValue={40} />;
}
```

`onChange` fires on every move, also while dragging. `onChangeEnd` fires once
the drag ends, and on every keyboard step: use it to save or to search.

### Ranges

Pass `[start, end]` as the value and the slider gets two thumbs. `minGap` keeps
them apart, like a price range at least $100 wide. `formatOptions` formats the
bubbles, the marks and what screen readers hear with `Intl.NumberFormat`, in
the provider's `locale`.

<Example name="slider/range" title="Slider · Price range" />

When the thumbs get so close that their bubbles would overlap, the bubbles
merge into one ("$650 – $800") between the thumbs, and split again as the thumbs
move apart.

Name the two thumbs for screen readers with `thumbLabels`, in your app's
language.

### Marks and custom text

`marks` draws ticks under the track, as numbers or `{ value, label }`.
`formatValue` writes the text yourself, for steps that aren't numbers.
`valueBubble="active"` shows the bubble only while the slider is used.

<Example name="slider/marks" title="Slider · Marks" />

### In a form

With a `name`, the value goes into a hidden input. A range sends both values
under the same name; read them with `getAll`.

## Personalities

- **Sticker:** the thumb lags a little behind the pointer, stretches in the
  direction you pull and sways when you let go, then lands with a wobble.
- **Retro:** the thumb hops from step to step, one frame each.
- **Comic:** a red ball that leaves speed lines when moved fast, a halftone fill
  and a speech bubble for the value.
- **Arcade:** the track is a row of LED blocks, the thumb a joystick ball, and
  the bubble a scoreboard that flashes on every step.

With reduced motion the thumb follows the pointer exactly, without lag or
stretch.

## Accessibility

- Every thumb is a `slider` with its value, minimum and maximum; the text read
  is the formatted value ("$200").
- `←` `↓` and `→` `↑` move one step, `Page Up` and `Page Down` a tenth of the
  range, `Home` and `End` jump to the ends. In right-to-left languages, `←` and
  `→` are reversed.
- In a range, each thumb's limits follow the other thumb and `minGap`, and
  screen readers hear those limits too.
- The focus ring shows for keyboard users only; dragging with a mouse doesn't
  leave one behind.
